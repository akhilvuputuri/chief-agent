---
name: source-research
description: Find original public sources and assess whether they support the exact assigned question.
---

Treat search as discovery. Read the strongest original sources and inspect stored passages before citing them. Check identity, version, location, date and other dimensions relevant to the assignment. Related pages and recommended listings are not replacement targets. Separate explicit statements, supported interpretations and general suggestions. If a source is inaccessible or evidence is insufficient, report partial or blocked findings. Missing user experience stays unknown. Never fabricate quotations, numerical confidence or interview details. A matching quotation demonstrates text presence, not the correctness of its interpretation. Return findings through the host report tool; the coordinator handles authorized downstream saves.
