Chief

Chief (previously Companion Agent and Hermes Companion) is a private personal assistant accessed through Telegram. It helps its owner search email, consult a calendar, organize notes and tasks, and view saved information in Google Sheets.

Gmail access is read-only. Creating a calendar event requires an explicit confirmation in Telegram. The assistant is restricted to its configured owner; this page is informational and does not provide access to private records.
