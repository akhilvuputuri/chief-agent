# 73 — Which harness mechanisms justify their cost?

Work date(s): 2026-10-09. Written/revised: 2026-10-09.
Status: research and proposed experiments; no measured Chief coding-quality or cost advantage.

## User-visible problem and preceding iteration

[Journal 71](71-coding-progress-recovery.md) addressed unproductive inspection and context loss. [Journal 72](72-coding-approval-brief.md) separates working evidence from the complete phone approval scope. A successful plan still does not establish end-to-end coding reliability or economical independent review. The next question is whether inexpensive models can complete small, well-specified changes using this harness, and whether three sequential roles justify their additional calls and latency.

## Evidence and diagnosis

Two owner-requested research agents examined original papers and first-party harness experiments. The [evidence and experiment design](../coding-model-efficiency.md) records models, task denominators, controls, historical prices and missing accounting. Context management has direct cheap-model cost evidence, with accuracy reversals under another thinking configuration. Stronger review can improve selected patch outcomes but does not establish equal-compute cost superiority; weak reviewers can damage good patches. Scouting results suggest a useful handoff can matter more than a routing layer. Extra agents are therefore an experimental policy rather than a demonstrated improvement.

Chief has one observed v2 planning completion, no observed complete coder/reviewer/PR production trajectory and an offline fixture/grader pack without a disposable inference adapter. Existing development cases have public source/grader material and are not an untouched holdout. No production role, model, allocation or permission changes follow from this research.

## Proposed implementation and verification

Prioritize the isolated inference adapter/accounting, guarded atomic edits with diagnostic feedback, focused evidence-rich handoffs, reviewer confusion/rework tests and state-preserving context comparisons. Compare cheap solo, stronger solo, cheap coder/strong reviewer and the current three-member squad on common frozen requirements. Keep common harness/tools/environment/ceilings, report actual usage, and use an equal-extra-repair control before attributing benefit to critique.

Count all attempts and total cost, including failed/review/rework/compute work. Separate infrastructure outcomes, unknown accounting, owner intervention, good patches damaged and bad patches approved. Small repeated development trials validate the adapter and expose variance; they cannot establish general capability or create independent tasks by repetition.
