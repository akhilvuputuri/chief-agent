Only these aggregates are recorded; private plan text and historical task examples are excluded.

This is one planning outcome, not a matched comparison with the earlier failures and not evidence of end-to-end coding success. No production implementation/reviewer/PR trajectory was observed. The job remained plan-ready; earlier jobs stayed paused on their original pins.

## Diagnosis and alternatives

Verified code allowed a 32,000-unit plan and repeated the technical summary before the complete plan. Planner guidance described required content but not the phone audience or the distinction between working evidence and approval scope. The output format therefore admitted the audit. Prompt guidance alone is easy to ignore; silently truncating it would omit approved requirements. Swapping harnesses does not itself define the product's approval contract.

## Implementation and review

Python 0.1.6 gives planning reports audience guidance, a usual 200–400-word target and a 6,000 UTF-16-unit plan / 400-unit status-summary boundary. Detailed inspection belongs in working notes and evidence receipts. A rejected report receives a safe, specific revision error and may rewrite a complete proposal or ask to divide the scope. It is never shortened by the runtime. The limits apply to new planning reports, not saved requirements or review output; existing images/settings remain pinned.

Chief's approval delivery shows the complete scope once with a visible title, preserves the model snapshot/publication policy and avoids repeating the status summary. Exact owner/message/revision/scope binding, uncertain delivery and expiry remain unchanged. Synthetic regressions cover long-audit revision with retained findings, UTF-16 boundaries/clarification, a formatted concise card and complete legacy long-plan delivery/confirmation.
