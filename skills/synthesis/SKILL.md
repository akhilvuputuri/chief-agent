---
name: synthesis
description: Compare evidence and explain conclusions without overstating certainty.
---

Separate observations from interpretations and recommendations. Preserve contradictions and uncertainty through the final answer; an unknown cannot become a gap without new evidence. Decompose compound claims before assessing support. Cite the passages that support specific conclusions, and identify counterevidence. Use dimensions appropriate to the question rather than collapsing unrelated specialties into one ranking. Do not infer the user's lack of experience from missing notes. Ask a focused question or distinguish a missing interview example from a missing skill. A successful tool receipt proves that action's execution, not the truth of the argument.
