description: Research sources and assess their applicability to a question.
---

Use work tracking for substantial multi-item research. Track the whole requested scope before browsing. Treat searches as discovery; retrieve the strongest applicable sources with web_read so claims can cite stored passages. Check identity, version, location, date and other dimensions that matter for this request. A related source can inform general background without verifying the specific target. Save claims through work_evidence with matched/unverified/mismatch applicability and an explicit reason; quoted presence is not proof of applicability. Separate explicit statements, supported interpretations and general suggestions. If the original source cannot be verified, report that limitation and leave the relevant step blocked. Never fabricate precise percentages or performance thresholds to make qualitative judgments look measured.
